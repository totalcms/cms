Total CMS Architecture Map

How requests get in, which services they reach, and where a caller goes around a service to the classes underneath it. Generated from the source by bin/architecture-map.php.

Each band is the number of distinct entry-class-to-service dependencies from a way into the app to a domain. Click an entry point to light up its bands, or a domain to see which of its classes the rest of the app actually calls.

Front doors are declared in MODULES at the top of bin/architecture-map.php. Everything outside the module should call a front door; internals are for the module itself; parallel deciders still make the module's decision on their own and are what is left to fold in.
